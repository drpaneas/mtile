# Code of Conduct

Mtile welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces.

- Be respectful and help people participate. Explain unfamiliar terminology.
- Critique code and ideas; do not attack people.
- Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Respect privacy, consent, and others' time.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct,
  impersonation, and publishing someone's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict
participation when behavior violates these expectations. The response should
reflect the severity and persistence of the behavior. Maintainers are held to
the same standards as other participants.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a maintainer through a private contact method they have made public
on their GitHub profile. If none is available, ask for a private contact
channel without posting the incident details or identifying other people.
Use GitHub's own reporting tools for platform-level abuse.

Reports should describe what happened and include relevant links if safe to
share. Maintainers should limit disclosure to what is needed to address the
concern and should explain any moderation decision to the people involved
when practical.
