Candidate:
Replace the generated gluNumberPow loop for proven numeric bases and literal
exponents -4 through 4 with a fixed sequence. Evaluate the base once, preserve
the helper's multiplication order and fixed-point wrapping, and use One.Div
for negative powers. In particular, x^2 becomes x.Mul(x), x^3 becomes
x.Mul(x.Mul(x)), and x^4 squares the stored x.Mul(x) result. Larger exponents
retain the helper. The candidate was removed after the target comparison.

Method:
